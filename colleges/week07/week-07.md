
# Week 7 - Consolidation

* Teaching method: Seminar
* Duration: 3 × 2 teaching hours

## Web Development Seminar

### Learning objectives

Students will:

* consolidate the knowledge and skills acquired during previous weeks;
* ask questions about topics they find challenging;
* further develop their portfolio assignments.

### Content

This seminar provides students with the opportunity to reinforce the concepts covered throughout the module.

Additional demonstrations may be provided on request, and previously covered topics can be revisited where necessary.
Students may also request the discussion of relevant supplementary topics.

### Preparation / Individual assignments

Complete all Week 7 assignments.
