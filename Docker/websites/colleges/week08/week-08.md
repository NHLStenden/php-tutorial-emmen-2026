# Week 8

* Teaching method: Seminar
* Duration: 3 × 2 teaching hours

## Web Development Seminar

### Learning objectives

By the end of this seminar, students will be able to independently develop a complete website using the knowledge and
skills acquired throughout the module.

### Content

The seminar focuses on final preparation for the practical assessment.

Topics include:

* examination tips;
* common programming mistakes;
* best practices;
* answering remaining questions.

### Preparation / Individual assignments

Complete and finalise all Week 8 assignments.
